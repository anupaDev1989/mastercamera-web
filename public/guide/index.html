Feature by feature

Every tab, explained

A closer look at what each part of Master Camera does — and how to get the most from it.

Camera

Shoot first, sort later with Rapid Mode

Open the app and start shooting immediately. Every photo and video lands in your library unsorted, ready to organize whenever you have a moment — perfect for fast-moving job sites.

  • Rapid Mode: one-tap photo & video capture, no setup.
  • Context Mode: assign a stack, tags, and notes as you shoot.
  • Optional GPS metadata overlay baked onto photos, set per stack.
  • Works fully offline, with Level & Compass and QR code overlays built in.
📸
Rapid Mode
One tap → straight to your library
Gallery

All your captures, and Stacks to organize them

The Gallery has two views you swipe between: All shows every capture in one scrollable grid, and Stacks are the projects you organize into — each with its own tags, custom fields, watermark, and metadata settings.

  • All: every photo and video, browsable by date or media type.
  • Stacks: one project per job, listing, or topic — fast to switch between.
  • Multi-select to batch-assign a stack or tags to several captures at once.
  • See markup and dictated-note indicators at a glance.
🖼️
All & Stacks
Swipe between everything and your projects
Detail view

Markup, dictation & rich metadata

Open any capture to preview it full-screen and enrich it. Draw or drop shapes directly on photos, dictate a note instead of typing, and edit every field in one place.

  • Photo markup with a pencil and shapes — circle, arrow, sticker, annotate.
  • Dictation: speech-to-text notes — talk instead of type.
  • Typed notes, tags, and stack assignment.
  • Custom fields shown right alongside the media.
✏️
Markup & Dictation
Annotate and dictate context for any shot
Stacks

Stacks & custom fields

Group captures into stacks — one per job, listing, or topic — and define your own custom fields with text or multiple-choice values to record exactly the data your work needs.

  • Create a stack for every job, listing, or topic.
  • Define custom fields per stack (Room #, Job Code, Status…) as text or multiple-choice.
  • Tags work across every stack for flexible cross-filtering.
  • Everything stays searchable — including custom field values.
📁
Stacks

Search

Find any shot in seconds

Type to search notes, tags, stack names, and custom field values at once, then narrow the results with filters so you always land on exactly the right capture.

  • Full-text search over notes, tags, stacks & custom values.
  • Filter by stack, tag, date range, and media type.
  • Filter to captures with a location or a note attached.
  • Results return in under a second, even on large libraries.
🔍

Settings

Private, offline-first — configured your way

Master Camera works entirely offline and keeps everything on your device — no account, no cloud sync, no third-party servers. Tune capture quality, branding, and how your library is laid out from Settings.

  • Choose whether Gallery opens to All or Stacks by default.
  • Turn on Timeline View, or switch Stacks between grid and list layout.
  • Set capture quality (HEIC/H.264) and toggle enhanced processing.
  • Manage tags, appearance (light/dark), and Master Camera Pro from one place.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about capturing and organizing with Master Camera.

Is Master Camera free to use?

Master Camera is free to download and use, with a generous set of stacks, tags, and custom fields included. Master Camera Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited stacks and tags, more custom fields, custom filename formatting, and Replicate.

Where are my photos and videos stored?

Your media is stored entirely on your device. Master Camera doesn't require an account and doesn't sync to any cloud or third-party server.

Is there cloud backup or sync across devices?

Not yet. Everything currently stays local to your device. Cloud backup is planned for a future update.

Does Master Camera work offline?

Yes. Master Camera is fully offline — capture, organize, mark up, and dictate notes with no connection required, any time.

Can I mark up photos and dictate notes?

Absolutely. Open any capture in the detail view to draw annotations or drop shapes with the pencil tool, and use speech-to-text dictation to add notes without typing.

How do stacks and custom fields work?

Stacks group your captures by job, listing, or topic. Each stack can define its own custom fields — text or multiple-choice — so you can record exactly the data your workflow needs, and every value is searchable. A stack can also carry its own watermark and metadata-overlay settings.

What devices are supported?

Master Camera is built for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Can I search for a specific photo later?

Yes. Search across notes, tags, stack names, and custom field values, then filter by stack, tag, date range, or media type to find any shot in seconds.
