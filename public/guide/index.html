Feature 1 · Camera

Rapid capture & Context capture

Two ways to shoot so you never have to choose between speed and detail.

Camera tab

Shoot first, sort later with Rapid Mode

Open the app and start shooting immediately. Every photo and video lands in your Inbox, ready to organize whenever you have a moment. Perfect for fast-moving job sites and busy shoots.

  • Rapid Mode: One-tap photo & video capture to Inbox.
  • Context Mode: Assign a project, tags, and notes at the moment of capture.
  • Optional location stamp for every capture (toggle on/off).
  • Works fully offline — captures sync later automatically.

Inbox tab

Sort at your own pace

The Inbox holds everything you capture in Rapid Mode. Clear it in bulk — select multiple items and assign them to a project, tag them, or add a note in one action. An empty Inbox means everything is filed.

  • See all unsorted captures in one scrollable grid.
  • Multi-select to batch-assign projects and tags.
  • Swipe into any item to enrich or edit it.
  • A clear goal: shrink your Inbox to zero.

Feature 3 · All Media

Browse your whole library

All tab

Every photo and video, beautifully organized

The All gallery shows your complete library, segmented by type and date. Smooth, lazy-loading thumbnails keep scrolling fast even across thousands of items.

  • Browse by date or media type (photo / video).
  • Fast, lazy-loaded thumbnails built for large libraries.
  • See markup and voice-note indicators at a glance.
  • Tap any item to open full detail and editing.

Search tab

Powerful search across everything

Type to search notes, tags, project names, and custom field values at once. Then narrow the results with filters so you always land on exactly the right capture.

Feature 7 · Settings & iCloud Sync

Private sync, your way

Settings tab

Offline-first with private iCloud sync

Master Camera works offline first and syncs privately through your own iCloud account — no third-party servers. Choose how the app launches and control your privacy from Settings.

  • Set your default launch view (Camera or Gallery).
  • Toggle location capture on or off.
  • See iCloud sync status, last sync time & storage usage.
  • Background sync keeps every device up to date automatically.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about capturing and organizing with Master Camera.

Is Master Camera free to use?

You can download Master Camera and start capturing right away. Check the App Store listing for the latest pricing and any premium options.

Where are my photos and videos stored?

Your media is stored on your device and synced privately through your own iCloud account using Apple's secure infrastructure. There are no third-party servers involved.

Does Master Camera work offline?

Yes. Master Camera is offline-first. You can capture, organize, mark up, and add voice notes with no connection — everything syncs automatically once you're back online.

Can I mark up photos and add voice notes?

Absolutely. Open any capture in the detail view to draw annotations with the pencil tool and record a voice note for spoken context.

How do projects and custom fields work?

Projects group your captures and can contain subfolders. Each project can define custom fields — text, number, date, yes/no, or pick-list — so you can record exactly the data your workflow needs, and every value is searchable.

What devices are supported?

Master Camera is built for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later, with seamless iCloud sync across your devices.

Can I search for a specific photo later?

Yes. Search across notes, tags, project names, and custom field values, then filter by project, date, media type, markup, voice note, or Inbox status to find any shot in seconds.
